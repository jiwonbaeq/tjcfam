TJC Fam privacy

Last updated October 7, 2026 · www.jiwonbaeq.com/fam

TJC Fam is a private scheduling tool that Jiwon Baeq uses to see and arrange the Google Calendars of a small team. It is not offered to the public, and only Google accounts on its allow-list can sign in.

What it accesses

When you sign in with Google, TJC Fam asks for your email address and permission to see and edit your Google Calendars. It uses that permission only to show calendar events in the app and to make the changes you make there (creating, moving, renaming and deleting events).

What it stores

  • A sign-in key from Google, kept encrypted in a cookie in your own browser so you stay signed in. It is not stored on any server or database.
  • Your display settings (lane names, colors and which calendar goes in which lane), kept in your browser's local storage.

Calendar events are read from Google when you open the app and are not copied or kept anywhere else.

Sharing

TJC Fam does not sell, share or transfer your data to anyone, and does not use it for advertising or for training AI models. Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Sign out from the app's settings, or remove TJC Fam at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Either one ends its access to your calendars.
